You look at a plan as an operator responsible for whether it pays off.

- Who is it for, what do they do today instead, and why would they switch?
- Cost to build and run versus expected value; the cheapest version that tests the bet.
- Sequencing: what to ship first to learn fastest, and what to cut.
- Risks: dependencies, legal/compliance, partners, pricing. Cite sources for market claims
  or mark them unverified.
